# Free imagery: sources, licences, verified links and search keywords

Every source below is free for commercial use without attribution (credit
is still good manners and we give it). Licences were read on 12 October 2026
from each site's own licence page. Always re-check the individual photo
page before print: a few photos on these sites are "editorial only" or
Unsplash+ (paid), and the site marks them.

Avoid: Getty, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Alamy and any "editorial use only"
image; anything found on Google Images or Pinterest without a licence page;
photos of identifiable people for an ad that implies they use the product
(Pexels forbids implied endorsement). Use people photos for atmosphere and
the app's own screens for the product.

## Search keywords that work

Use these on Pexels, Unsplash and Pixabay. Add "Nigeria" or a city name
(Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Jos, Enugu, Kano) to filter out generic stock.

- Dishes: `jollof rice`, `egusi soup`, `fried rice chicken Nigeria`, `amala ewedu`, `pounded yam`, `suya`, `small chops`, `puff puff`, `moi moi`, `meat pie`, `chin chin`, `akara`, `pepper soup`, `ofada rice`, `Nigerian cake`
- Places: `buka`, `mama put`, `Nigerian restaurant`, `canteen Lagos`, `food vendor Nigeria`, `street food Lagos`, `Nigerian bakery`, `catering Nigeria`, `party food Nigeria`
- Buying: `Nigerian market`, `Mile 12 market`, `Bodija market`, `tomato seller Nigeria`, `pepper market`, `rice bag market`, `palm oil`, `yam market`, `plantain seller`
- Cooking: `cooking pot outdoor Nigeria`, `African cooking fire`, `big pot rice`, `frying plantain`, `kitchen Nigeria`
- People: `Nigerian woman entrepreneur`, `small business owner Africa phone`, `woman using phone Nigeria`, `market woman smiling`
- Money and receipts (for abstract posts): `naira notes`, `POS machine Nigeria`, `receipt`, `calculator notebook`

## Illustrations and icons

- **unDraw** (https://undraw.co/illustrations): set the colour picker to `#1F7A52` before downloading. Good searches: `cooking`, `chef`, `shopping`, `receipt`, `savings`, `calculator`, `mobile app`, `online payments`, `business analytics`, `wallet`.
- **Storyset** (https://storyset.com, free with attribution, Freepik licence): `food`, `cooking`, `street food`, `finance app`. Needs a credit line; prefer unDraw where possible.
- **Lucide** (https://lucide.dev) is the icon family the app already draws on; keep 1.75 px stroke, rounded joins, 20–24 px.
- **Phosphor** (https://phosphoricons.com) for marketing where a bolder "fill" style is wanted.
- **Flagpack / Twemoji** for the Nigerian flag if ever needed (prefer the words "Built in Nigeria").

## Mockups and devices

- **Shots.so** (https://shots.so) and **Mockuphone** (https://mockuphone.com): free phone frames for app screenshots.
- **Screely** (https://screely.com): browser frames.
- The renders in `09-uiux/renders/` already carry a neutral phone frame drawn in CSS; no third-party mockup is needed for most posts.
