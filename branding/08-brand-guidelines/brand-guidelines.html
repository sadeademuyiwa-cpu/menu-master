Brand guidelines

How Menu Master NG looks and sounds, on one page. For anyone making a screen, a post, a flyer or a sentence in the product. Version 1.0 · 12 October 2026.

01The idea

Nigerian food businesses should never lose money without knowing it. Menu Master turns an owner's own market receipts into the true cost of every dish, the right price, and the profit on every sale. On a phone, in plain language, from their numbers and nobody else's.

Positioning

The money-truth app for Nigerian food businesses.

Brand line

Know your cost. Know your price. Know your profit.

Personality

The sharp, warm friend who used to work at a bank and now runs her own kitchen. Straight-talking, practical, calm about money.

Ada · buka / rice seller

Sells plates daily. Buys by the paint and the basket. Needs: what one plate costs, today.

Tunde · caterer

Quotes per head on WhatsApp. Needs: a price for 100 guests that doesn't lose money.

Kemi · baker

Cakes and small chops by the order. Needs: the cost of one cake with box and labour.

02Logo: the Rising Bowl

A bowl whose inside is the letter M, on a plinth, with three gold bars rising from it. The bowl is the food; the bars are the money going up. Supplied by the owner and rebuilt as vector so it works from a 16 px favicon to a shop sign.

Horizontal lockup · headers, email, letterhead
Reversed · on Forest Green
All-white · on a photo with a Forest tint
Mark alone
Mono · receipts, one-colour print
App icon · stores, WhatsApp
Favicon · 32 and 16 px

Do

Use the stacked lockup wherever there is room. Keep clear space of two-thirds of the mark's height around it. Minimum 24 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print.

Do

On green or a photo, use the reversed or all-white version. On receipts, the mono version.

Don't

Recolour, rotate, outline, add a shadow or gradient, change the bar heights, set the wordmark in another face, or put the colour lockup on a photo.

Proportions

60% white and canvas · 25% ink and stone · 10% greens · 5% gold.

Gold is an accent

One gold thing per screen: a badge, the NG, an emphasised word, a chart's second series. Never body text on white. Never next to an amber warning.

Accessibility

Every text colour here passes WCAG AA on white (Forest 7.8, Kitchen 5.3, Gold Text 5.1, Stone 5.1). Gold on Forest is for large text only.

04Type

Two free Google Fonts. Plus Jakarta Sans (600–800) for display and headings: confident and friendly. Inter (400–700) for everything else: the most legible UI face on a cheap Android screen, with tabular figures and the Naira sign.

Rules

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. No exclamation marks. Tabular numbers for money. The ₦ is tight to the number, thousands separated, two decimals only where kobo matter. A missing number is a word ("not known yet"), never 0. Minimum 15 px in the app, 16 px in marketing. Max 65 characters a line.

05Voice and tone

Second person, present tense, plain Nigerian English. Dish, not recipe variant. What you keep, not gross margin. A paint of rice, not 4.0 kg. The owner is running a business; never "cheap", "poor" or "struggling".

06Imagery

The owner's world as it is: pots, plates, markets, hands, phones. Natural light and warm food colours. No stock business people, no laptops on marble, no chef hats. Overlay with Forest Green at 70–85%, never a black gradient. Verified free sources and licences are in 06-imagery/free-image-sources.md.

08Never

Never show ₦0 for a missing number

The governing rule of the product is the first rule of the brand: the owner's own data, or nothing.

Never imply a real person endorses us

Stock photos set the mood; they are not testimonials. Real customers only with written consent.

Never look like a spreadsheet or a bank

No dense grids on phones, no navy, no gradients, no "fintech bro". Warm food colours, calm green, one gold.
