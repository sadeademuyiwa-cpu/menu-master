# Menu Master NG — Brand Strategy

The fixed part of the brand. If a design, a post or a sentence cannot be
traced back to something on this page, it does not belong to Menu Master.

## 1. Purpose

**Nigerian food businesses should never lose money without knowing it.**

Thousands of bukas, caterers, bakers and food vendors are busy every day
and still end the month with nothing left, because the price on the board
was set by guesswork and the market moved. Menu Master exists so that the
owner always knows, from their own receipts, what a plate costs, what to
charge, and what they kept.

## 2. Positioning statement

> For Nigerian food business owners who sell food every day and do their
> sums in their head, Menu Master NG is the phone app that turns market
> receipts into the true cost of every dish, the right price and the profit
> on every sale. Unlike notebooks, spreadsheets and restaurant software built
> for laptops, Menu Master uses only the owner's own numbers, in the units
> they actually buy (a bag, a paint, a derica, a crate), and says it in
> plain language.

One line: **the money-truth app for Nigerian food businesses.**

## 4. Brand values

1. **Your numbers, not ours.** Costs come only from what the owner entered. We never show an average, a benchmark or a guess. A missing number stays missing and we say so. (This is the product's governing rule, and it is also the brand's first value.)
2. **Plain Nigerian English.** Dish, not recipe variant. What you keep, not gross margin. A paint of rice, not 4.0 kg.
3. **Respect for a busy owner.** Four taps to a sale. Nothing to learn before the first result. The phone is the whole product.
4. **Calm about money.** No alarm red everywhere, no confetti. A clear green when things are good, a warm amber when they need attention, a plain sentence either way.

## 5. Personality

bank, now runs her own kitchen, and will tell you straight what your jollof
costs. She is:

## 6. Tagline and messaging

**Brand line (keep):** Know your cost. Know your price. Know your profit.

**Headline for ads and the landing page:** Know what every plate really costs — and price it to profit.

**Short lines for social, stickers, WhatsApp status:**
- Every plate, priced to profit.
- Busy is not the same as profitable.
- Tomatoes went up. Did your price?
- Your receipts already know your price.
- Stop copying the shop next door.

**Three key messages** (every piece of marketing carries at least one):
1. *True cost per plate* from what you actually paid, in the units you buy.
2. *A price that pays*: see profit per plate before you change the board.
3. *Prices that keep up*: record a purchase and every dish is recosted.

**Proof points** (only ever claim what is true today): 14 days free, no card
needed, works on any phone, payments by Paystack, founding price for the
first 100 businesses.

## 7. Voice and tone

Voice is constant; tone shifts with the moment.

| Moment | Tone | Example from the product |
|---|---|---|
| Teaching | Plain, concrete, one idea | "Your market purchases, the way you buy them: a 50 kg bag of rice, a crate of eggs, a paint bucket of pepper." |
| Good news | Warm, specific, no exclamation marks | "At ₦3,300 you keep ₦1,460 a plate." |
| Needs attention | Calm, names the fix | "You are making money, but less than you planned. Charging ₦1,450 would reach your target." |
| Missing data | Honest, never fills the gap | "Cost unknown: no price for pepper yet." (never ₦0) |
| Errors | Say what happened and what to do, in that order | "That didn't save. Check your connection and try again." |
| Legal and billing | Short sentences, no hedging | "Nothing is deleted. You can still open and read everything you entered." |

Rules:
- Second person, present tense. "You keep", not "the user retains".
- Naira sign tight to the number: ₦1,450. Thousands separated. Two decimals only where kobo matter.
- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. "Save price", not "SAVE PRICE".
- No exclamation marks. No emoji in the product; sparing emoji in WhatsApp marketing is fine.
- Nigerian spelling and words: colour, flavour, paint, derica, buka, small chops.
- Never "cheap", "poor", "struggling". The owner is running a business.

## 8. Name

**Menu Master NG.** Pronounced as written. "Menu Master" in running text
after the first mention. "NG" says Nigeria and distinguishes the product
from unrelated apps of the same name abroad. Never "MenuMaster" (one word),
"MMNG" or "Menu Master Nigeria".

Domain: menumasterng.com. Handle to secure on Instagram, X, TikTok,
Facebook: @menumasterng.

## 9. Brand architecture

One brand, two plans. The plans are named in the owner's words and are
features of Menu Master, not sub-brands:

- **Costing** — what every dish costs, prices, profit per plate.
- **Costing + Sales** — plus orders, WhatsApp receipts, customers, reports.

"Founding member" is a price, not a tier; it gets a badge, not a logo.
